# v1.1 — List pagination showed only the first 15 records

**Version:** v1.1
**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Branch:** `developement`
**Commit:** `bf6c95b`
**Type:** Bug fix

---

## Summary

The Payslip, Certificates and Leave Entitlements pages only showed the first
15 staff. The footer read **"10 of 15"** across 2 pages when the backend had
**59** records across 4 pages.

## Cause

Every list endpoint is paginated server-side (15 rows per page). These three
pages called their endpoint without a `page` parameter, kept only
`response.data.data`, and ignored the `pagination` block. They then split those
15 rows into pages of 10 in the browser, so everything after server page 1
never appeared and nothing reported an error.

## Affected pages

| Page               | Endpoint              | Before   | After    |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | -------- | -------- |
| Payslip            | `/payrolls`           | 15 of 59 | 59 of 59 |
| Certificates       | `/user-certificates`  | 15 of 59 | 59 of 59 |
| Leave entitlements | `/leave-entitlements` | 15 of 59 | 59 of 59 |

## Fix

- Added `lib/api/fetch-all-pages.ts`. `fetchAllPages()` loads page 1, reads
  `last_page`, fetches the remaining pages in parallel and returns the
  combined rows.
- `payrollApi.getPayrollUsers`, `certificateApi.getCertificateUsers` and
  `leaveEntitlementApi.getLeaveEntitlements` now use it. No page component
  changes were needed.
- Added `pagination?: Pagination` to the payroll and certificate response
  types.

These pages keep paging in the browser because their department, branch,
status and search filters run in the browser and need the full list.

## Audit of other lists

Every other listing page was checked and needed no change:

- **Server-paged correctly:** claims, leave requests, overtime, users,
  announcements, upcoming events, movements, audit log, departments,
  positions, offices, roles, movement types and leave policies. Each sends
  `page` and shows the server's `pagination.total`.
- **Dropdowns (`/lookup/*`):** not paginated; they return the full list.

## Verification

- `tsc --noEmit` passes and the changed files pass lint.
- Dev server log shows each of the three pages requesting pages 1–4 from the
  API, all returning 200.

## Prevention

- New `PAGINATION.md` documents the two correct ways to consume a list
  endpoint (server-side paging, or `fetchAllPages` + client paging) and a
  checklist for new lists.
- `CLAUDE.md` now states that every list endpoint is paginated and points to
  `PAGINATION.md`.

## Notes

- The backend honours `?size=N` (not `per_page`), but it isn't used as the fix
  because rows past the limit would be dropped silently again.
- Fetching every page suits staff-sized lists. If one of these grows into the
  hundreds, add the filters to the backend and switch that page to
  server-side paging.

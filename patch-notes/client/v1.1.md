# HRMS Update — Version 1.1

**Release date:** 1 November 2026

---

## What's fixed

### Complete staff lists on Payslip, Certificate and Leave Entitlement

Some list pages were only showing the first 15 staff members. The page count
and record total at the bottom of these pages were also lower than they
should have been.

These pages now show **every staff member**, and the totals at the bottom are
correct:

- **Payslip**
- **Certificate**
- **Leave Entitlement**

Search and filters on these pages now cover all staff, not just the first 15.

## Other pages

We checked every other list in the system, including Expenses, Leave,
Overtime, Users, Announcements, Events, Movements and the Configuration
pages. They were already showing complete records and needed no changes.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. The fix applies automatically. If a page still looks incomplete, refresh
your browser. If the problem continues, please contact us and tell us which
page you were on.

## Your data

This was a display issue only. No records were lost, changed or deleted.
Every payslip, certificate and leave balance was saved correctly the whole
time; some of them simply weren't appearing in the list.

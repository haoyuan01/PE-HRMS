# HRMS Update — Version 1.2

**Release date:** 3 November 2026

---

## What's new

### Filter leave requests by month

The Leave Requests page now has a **Filters** button next to the My List and
Staff List tabs. Choose a month and a year to see only the leave requests for
that month. The month you picked is shown next to the button, and **Reset**
takes you back to all months.

The summary boxes at the top of the page follow the month you choose too.

## What's fixed

### Rejected requests no longer appear for the General Manager

The General Manager's staff list was showing leave requests that had
already been rejected and didn't need their attention. The list now shows only
the requests that should reach the General Manager.

### Summary boxes now match the list

On the Leave Requests page, the four boxes at the top (Total, Pending,
Approved and Rejected) sometimes showed different numbers from the requests
listed below them. They now count exactly the same requests as the list, for
the tab and month you are viewing.

### Staff lists opened from the dashboard

Clicking **View more** under Recent Leave Request or Recent Claim Request on
the dashboard sometimes opened a staff list with no records in it, even
though there were requests to show. These lists now load correctly every
time.

### More accurate lists and totals

"My" and "Staff" lists on the Leave Requests page, the Expenses Claim page and
the dashboard are now prepared directly by the system. Each list shows the
right records for your role, and the totals at the bottom of each page match
the records in the list.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. The changes apply automatically. If a page still looks out of date,
refresh your browser. If something still looks wrong, please contact us and
tell us which page you were on.

## Your data

No records were lost, changed or deleted. Every leave request and expense
claim was saved correctly the whole time.
